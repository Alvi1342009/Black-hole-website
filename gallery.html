🌌 Real Images from Space

For many years, black holes could only be imagined through computer simulations. Today, powerful observatories such as the Event Horizon Telescope (EHT), NASA, ESA, and other scientific missions have allowed us to capture incredible images and data from these mysterious objects.

The first black hole ever photographed by the Event Horizon Telescope in 2019.

🌟 Did You Know?

📸 The First Black Hole Photo

The first-ever image of a black hole, M87*, was released on 10 April 2019 by the Event Horizon Telescope collaboration.

🌌 Not a Direct Photo

The famous black hole images show glowing gas around the black hole, not the black hole itself. The dark center is its shadow.

🔭 Many Telescopes, One Giant Telescope

The Event Horizon Telescope combined radio telescopes around the world to create an Earth-sized virtual telescope.

🛰️ Space Missions Help

NASA's Chandra, Hubble, James Webb, and other observatories continue to study black holes across the universe.
