👤 Meet the Creator

The person behind Black Hole Explorer.

👩‍🚀 About the Creator

Hi! I'm Alvi Fatima, a Class 12 student from Kashmir, India, with a strong interest in physics, mathematics, astronomy, and space science. I have always been fascinated by the questions that lie beyond what we can see—from the birth and evolution of stars to the extreme physics of black holes.

Alongside academics, I enjoy public speaking, anchoring, writing, creative design, and scientific communication. I have had the opportunity to anchor school events and an international auditorium event, participate in academic competitions and Olympiads, and take part in activities involving essay writing, poetry, poster designing, art, and news reading.

I created Black Hole Explorer to combine my interest in space with my interest in technology and communication. My goal was to take a topic that can seem intimidating and explain it in a way that is visual, simple, interactive, and enjoyable.

🔭 My Interests

🌌 Astronomy & Space

Exploring the universe, black holes, stars, galaxies, and the physics that governs extreme cosmic environments.

📐 Physics & Mathematics

Understanding the mathematical ideas behind the physical world and using them to explore questions about the universe.

🎤 Communication

Public speaking, anchoring, writing, and making complex ideas easier for other people to understand.

🔭 Why I Created This Website

Black holes have always been one of the most fascinating mysteries of the universe. I wanted to create a place where anyone—even someone learning about black holes for the first time—could explore questions such as:

  • 🌌 What exactly is a black hole?
  • ⭐ How are black holes formed?
  • 🕳️ What happens beyond the event horizon?
  • ⏳ Why does time behave differently near a black hole?
  • 🌊 What are gravitational waves?
  • 🔭 How do scientists study objects that cannot be seen directly?

🚀 My Journey With This Project

Building Black Hole Explorer has been more than simply creating a website. It has been an opportunity to bring together my interests in astronomy, physics, mathematics, communication, and technology.

While building the project, I learned how to structure webpages with HTML, design them with CSS, add interactivity with JavaScript, organize scientific information, find appropriate visuals, and present complicated concepts through storytelling and interactive learning.

Most importantly, this project reminded me that curiosity is often where scientific exploration begins.

💡 What I Learned

Creating this website allowed me to explore astronomy while also learning about web design, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, research, visual communication, and scientific storytelling. The project taught me that science does not have to be difficult to be fascinating—it just needs to be explained in the right way.
